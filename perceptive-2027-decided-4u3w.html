03 · The base plan
How the $3,500 HSA works
In-network amounts. Preventive care is covered at no charge, before the deductible.
1
Company HSA money
$1,750 single
$2,750 family

Goes into your HSA. It is yours to keep, and unused money rolls over every year.

➜
2
Deductible
$3,500 per person
$7,000 per family

You pay the cost of care until it is met. Your HSA can pay it tax-free.

➜
3
Coinsurance
You pay 30%

After the deductible, the plan pays 70% for most services. Most prescriptions have set copays.

➜
4
Out-of-pocket max
$8,700 per person
$17,400 per family

The most you pay in a year. After that, covered care is paid at 100%.

Each person meets their own deductible

The deductible is embedded. Once one family member reaches $3,500, the plan starts sharing costs for that person, even if the family hasn’t reached $7,000.

Example: if one family member needs care, the company’s $2,750 covers all but $750 of that person’s deductible.

The most you could pay, after company HSA money
Single$6,950
Family$14,650

Out-of-pocket max minus company HSA money ($8,700 − $1,750; $17,400 − $2,750). No referrals are needed to see a specialist.

04 · The buy-up
The POS buy-up: same plan, wider network
For anyone who lives or gets regular care outside Michigan.
What stays the same
  • The same $3,500 HSA design: deductible, 30% coinsurance and out-of-pocket max
  • The same company HSA money: $1,750 single / $2,750 family
  • The same Priority Health doctors in Michigan
  • No referrals needed to see a specialist
What the POS adds
  • Care outside Michigan through participating Cigna providers, paid at the in-network level
  • Coverage for out-of-network providers, with a higher deductible ($7,000 / $14,000) and 50% coinsurance
  • 90 days of continuity of care for new members already in treatment
Who pays: the company pays its share of the HMO premium for everyone. An employee who picks the POS pays the difference. Across the group, the POS premium runs about 9% more than the HMO. We will show each person’s exact cost at open enrollment.
Sources: Priority Health 2027 SBC, $3,500 HSA POS (p.1–3); PH “POS is the new PPO” flyer; Perceptive_All-HSA_Options.xlsx (POS buy-up)Perceptive Controls 2027 Health Plan · 5

Amaze Health

Your Amaze Health support helps members find care, sort out prescriptions and answer billing and insurance questions.

Drug-maker copay programs

Many manufacturers offer copay programs that can help pay drug costs before the deductible is met. Ask Amaze, your pharmacy or us before your first fill.
