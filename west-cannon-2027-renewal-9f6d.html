A closer look

West Michigan Partners could be a really good fit

It keeps you with Priority Health, saves about $7,800 a year, and lowers the first-tier deductible to $1,750.

Why we like it

  • Same carrier, so the change is easy for staff
  • −$650.44 a month versus the renewal
  • Half the tier-1 deductible of G351 ($1,750 vs $3,500)
  • Still HSA-qualified, so the church’s HSA approach carries over

The one thing to check first

  • It’s a narrower network built around Corewell Health
  • Anyone who sees doctors outside Corewell should check them first
  • Care outside the main tier costs more (tier 2: 50% after a $5,250 deductible)
  • After the deductible, staff pay 20% rather than nothing
Our suggestion: if this one interests the committee, we’ll quietly check each family’s doctors before anything is decided.

Children’s dental

Pediatric dental: what’s required

Health reform treats children’s dental as an essential benefit, so Priority Health asks every small group to have a certified standalone children’s plan in place.

What the church needs to do

  • Offer a certified standalone pediatric dental plan (today: Delta Dental)
  • Confirm it on Priority Health’s renewal form
  • Cost today: $33.42 per child per month, billed for up to 3 children per family

What’s optional

  • Enrollment is optional: no one has to sign up
  • There’s no minimum number of families who must enroll
  • It can simply sit alongside your other benefits as an extra just for kids
How Beam fits: Beam is the more complete benefit and covers the whole family. Beam doesn’t offer the certified children’s plan, so the Delta plan stays on offer either way.
