A monthly wellbeing benefit with care through Amaze Health, on top of the health plan you already offer. It doesn't replace your health plan or the doctor your people love.

11 · Alternative path
The strategy: everyone on an HSA plan
HSA and out-of-state access are not either/or. Priority Health offers the same HSA plan on an HMO network or on a point-of-service (POS) network.
How it would work
  • HSA HMO for everyone who gets care in Michigan
  • HSA POS buy-up only for those who need out-of-state access (Cigna at the in-network level)
  • Company HSA money for all: $1,700 single / $3,500 family
  • Far better than last year's traditional G20: $4,000 family deductible, $18,000 family out-of-pocket max, no HSA money
How the market works: in small-group ACA rating, the price is the price, set by age and location, not health. Your levers are plan design and contributions.

One caveat: corporate status

If Perceptive is an S-corp and the owner holds more than 2%, his HSA contributions can't go through payroll pre-tax. He deducts them on his personal return instead, with no FICA savings. Corporate status is not yet confirmed; check with your CPA.

Why it makes more sense
  • Lower premium: $1,931.00 vs $2,017.66 a month
  • Deductible covered: the company's $3,500 pays the whole $3,500 family deductible
  • Tax savings on his own money: each $1,000 he puts in saves $350 to $400 at a 35% to 40% tax rate, even if it goes straight in and straight out for care he already expects
  • His to keep: unused money rolls over every year and grows tax-free
  • Lower worst case: $10,000 family out-of-pocket max vs $18,000
  • Same access: keeps the out-of-state POS network
