04 — Market Check
One Thing BCBSM Can Do That PH Can't
We shopped Blue Cross. On rate, the two carriers are neck-and-neck — and we still prefer Priority Health's service. But there's one structural difference worth knowing about: broad-network choice for your in-state employees.
Priority Health Today
Michigan staff are locked to the narrow network
Everyone in-state is on the WMP narrow network (great Corewell access, but narrow). PH's broad POS is out-of-state-only — an in-state employee who wants broader access has no option.
What BCBSM Allows
A narrow plan AND a broad plan — open to everyone
Offer a low-cost PCP Focus HMO (narrow) alongside a Blue Elect POS (broad, national BlueCard) — available to all employees. Each person picks the lane that fits.
Want to keep your doctors / go anywhere?
Choose the broad POS. Costs more · 20% coinsurance — but full freedom.
Want the lowest cost?
Choose PCP Focus. Lowest premium — but may need to change doctors & get specialist referrals.

04 — On the Radar
Ideas Worth Exploring — Now or Down the Road
Beyond the fully-insured renewal, three strategies we keep pricing each cycle. None is the move for 8/1 — but each could fit as PRTS evolves, and we want them on your radar.
Defined Contribution
ICHRA
PRTS sets a monthly stipend; each employee buys their own marketplace plan and is reimbursed pre-tax. Predictable cost, no claims risk.
Where it stands
We ran the numbers — individual rates run ~20% above small-group today. Not attractive now, but the market's in flux; could shift in the next year or two. Re-check annually.
Cost-Sharing Community
Sedera Health Share
A faith-aligned medical cost-sharing community (not insurance). Members share medical costs at a lower monthly contribution — a fit for healthier individuals.
Where it stands
Could be offered as a voluntary option for healthier employees who want a lower-cost alternative alongside the group plan. Worth exploring for the right subset.
Self-Funded Lite
Level Funding
Fixed monthly payment with stop-loss protection — a healthy group keeps unused claims dollars instead of subsidizing the community pool.
Where it stands
Don't qualify this year — a few older, higher-cost members add risk. But the group is young (avg age 32); as those members reach Medicare/retire, this gets attractive. We re-check every renewal.
These stay on the radar. We'll keep pricing all three each renewal cycle so PRTS always knows the alternatives — and we move when the math actually wins.

05 — Our Recommendation
Stay the Course — and Add One Enhancement
We pressure-tested the alternatives. None beats what you already have. So we recommend keeping your program intact and adding one enhancement that genuinely improves the employee experience.
What We Evaluated — and Set Aside (for now)
Blue Cross (BCBSM)
Rates comparable — not enough savings to justify a move. Switching would disrupt an estimated 15–25% of employees' primary care doctors (narrow PCP Focus), just to add broad-network access.
Level Funding
Group doesn't qualify this year (a few high-cost members). We'll keep checking every renewal — gets attractive as the group ages favorably.
ICHRA
Individual rates run ~20% above small-group today — not attractive now, but could shift in the next year or two.
What We Recommend for FY27
✓
Medical — stay with Priority Health. Same plans, WMP network, Corewell access. Update rates, keep contributions steady.
✓
Ancillary — stay with Beam. Dental absorbs Beam's 8.8% block increase (+$2,433/yr); vision, life, and the Hartford disability (2-yr guarantee) hold flat. Not worth disrupting over $2,433.
+
Enhancement — add Amaze Health. Concierge virtual care for every employee & their family. The one meaningful upgrade this year.
→
Next year — the wellbeing program. The path to make Amaze self-sustaining (more on that ahead).
Bottom line: everything stays the same — we just add a meaningful enhancement. Low disruption, better experience, and we keep watching level funding and the individual market for the year they swing in your favor.
